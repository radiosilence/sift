- **A refusing MusicBrainz is reported in seconds, not minutes.** A request used to retry for about six minutes while holding the pacing gate, so every import queued behind an outage waited through its own round. It now retries for up to 30 seconds, honouring `Retry-After`, and the client then fails requests without sending them until the requested wait is over, so an embedder can put its imports off and try again later.
